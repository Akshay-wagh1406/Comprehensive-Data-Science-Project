# Business Report

# Executive Summary

This project focuses on solving major business challenges using machine learning and analytics.

---

# Problem Statement

Businesses struggle with:
- Customer retention
- Revenue forecasting
- House valuation
- Sales optimization

---

# Key Insights

## Customer Churn
- Customers with short contracts are more likely to churn.
- High monthly charges increase churn probability.

## House Prices
- Location significantly impacts pricing.
- Larger houses have higher value.

## Sales Analytics
- Certain regions generate higher sales.
- Seasonal trends impact revenue.

---

# Recommendations

## Customer Retention
- Introduce loyalty programs.
- Improve customer support.

## House Pricing
- Invest in premium locations.
- Renovate older properties.

## Sales Improvement
- Increase focus on high-performing regions.
- Improve marketing during low-sales months.

---

# Conclusion

Machine learning and analytics provide valuable insights that improve business decision-making.
